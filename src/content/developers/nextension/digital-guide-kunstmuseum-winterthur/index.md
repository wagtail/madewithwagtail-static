The digital guide platform for Kunstmuseum Winterthur augments the physical onsite installation with spoken audio materials and background information about the locations for visitors. As part of the permanent exhibition in the Villa Flora house (https://digital.kmw.ch/en/villa-flora/), visitors can experience augmented reality on selected artworks using kiosk tablets. The whole site is driven by Wagtail, which is also the central feed for the kiosk tablets and AR assets.
