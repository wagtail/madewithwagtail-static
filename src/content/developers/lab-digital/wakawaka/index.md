WakaWaka is a global social enterprise that develops and sells portable solar products for the more than a billion people living without access to electricity and the billions seeking renewable energy solutions. WakaWaka means “shine bright” in Swahili. For every WakaWaka purchased, a solar light is donated to a family living without access to electricity. Having energy is a necessity for everyone, but it’s not available for everyone. WakaWaka makes it possible to have access to energy anytime at anyplace. In a short period of time Lab Digital expanded the website for WakaWaka and created completed a re-design.
