_La chambre des visiteurs_ (“Visitors’ Room”) is a website allowing the public from the museum of Fine Arts of Rouen to vote for the works to be exhibited soon. This is part of an event called _Le Temps des collections_ from the _Réunion des Musées de la Métropole de Rouen_ (“Union of the Museums from the Metropolis of Rouen”).

We made the 2016 edition of this website during August 2016. It has been since replaced since August 2017.
