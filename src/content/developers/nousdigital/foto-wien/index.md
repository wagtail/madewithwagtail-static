C/O Vienna is a platform for people from Vienna and their ideas, their projects, and their stories. Regardless of whether they are creative, brave, beautiful, bizarre, famous, or average, the world should hear about them. Read interviews, reviews and stories about Vienna’s most charismatic minds, places, and projects. Together with C/O Vienna and Seite zwei, we developed the essential blog-like information structure, wrapped it in a cool design by Seite zwei, and gave it a robust CMS. The result is a mobile guide to creative Vienna.
