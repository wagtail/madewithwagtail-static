Girl Effect builds youth brands and mobile platforms to empower girls to change their lives.

GE’s new visually striking Wagtail website is a gateway into their youth brands, platforms and programmes - from apps that build skills, to TV dramas that explore vital issues, to magazines written and distributed by girls.
