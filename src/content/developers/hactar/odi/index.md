ODI (formally the Overseas Development Institute) is a global think tank, researching and influencing policy and ideas for people and planet.

It's a resource and information heavy site with multiple user groups covering a huge range of topics and issues, from Covid to Climate Change.

Content centres on policy, research and opinion related to specific themes, with a focus on the organisation as experts.

Features include automated pages built on a tag, 'trending' topics and a people director to showcase expert voices.
