Den Braven dispatches millions of canisters and cartridges of sealants, foams, aerosols and other adhesives to construction enthusiasts all around the world.

Lukkien developed Den Braven's new multi-language corporate website, but the websites real power will be found in the intelligent way of importing their products. Thanks to this clever importer using data from their PIM and DAM, all information is ready at hand for their clients online and as a generated PDF file.
