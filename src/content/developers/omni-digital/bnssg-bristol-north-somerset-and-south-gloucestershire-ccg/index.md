As part of their 'Healthier Together' Sustainable Transformation Partnership, Omni helped Bristol, North Somerset and South Gloucestershire CCGs merge their sites, to create a stronger and clearer commissioning voice for the local area.

Wagtail has enabled us to utilise Django's fantastic application framework to build manageable, modular apps which are available to the wider Wagtail community. Wagtail's user-focused admin area has empowered NHS staff to provide better content to the public, improving the interaction between the NHS and the local community.
